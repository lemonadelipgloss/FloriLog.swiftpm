# FloriLog- Swift Student Challenge 2026 Submission
Journalling and daily reflections are a great release and an excellent way to store your memories to reminisce about later. But it can be quite daunting to even just begin it as a habit. The idea of sitting in front of a blank notebook and writing down an entire page every single day can be quite intimidating.

I stumbled upon the Victorian concept of floriography, or the “language of flowers”, about 6 years ago. The way I discovered it is admittedly quite unromantic (I was a bored 12-year-old scrolling around on Pinterest), but floriography is nothing short of what is possibly the most romantic thing I’ve ever come across in my life. I’ve always been fascinated by things like Morse code, Argots and anagrams, reading and learning about puzzles and ciphers fill me with ridiculous amounts of joy and excitement. And to find one this colorful with so many aspects and meanings to discover, I had truly struck gold. I was obsessed, scouring online libraries and websites (and more Pinterest boards) for anything and everything I could learn about this language. 

At the beginning of 2026, I made a New Year’s resolution to write daily reflections. Except instead of writing in a notebook, I decided to use sticky notes so I could fold them and keep them in a jar. And unlike most of my other resolutions, this one actually stuck!  I realized that having something tangible, that visibly grows over time is excellent motivation that make you want to stay consistent.

As I sat, brainstorming in the perfect notebook I picked out for ideas, I realized I could combine my newest good habit with my favorite hyperfixation. And so this was how FloriLog came to be.

<img width="1221" height="877" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/80760203-e3c6-4661-b262-667def259125" />

FloriLog uses different flowers to represent different moods. For this app I chose yellow tulip (happy), purple hyacinth (sad), tiger lily (angry), lotus flower (calm), and chamomile (tired).

<img width="759" height="691" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9b16852e-8d07-4897-8709-dd1fd46b7d2b" />

Every day users can pick out a flower that represents their mood throughout that day, and can write a journal entry to go with it. 

<img width="2752" height="2064" alt="screen1" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e9e8ba93-f688-425b-a625-62a9e99b992a" />

Each log is saved in a personal garden, that grows over time.

<img width="2752" height="2064" alt="screen2" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/34a51c20-9d32-4106-a7ab-89f473212b3e" />

At the end of each week, users can see their week summarized into a bouquet, which can be saved and viewed later in the bouquet gallery. 

<img width="2752" height="2064" alt="screen3" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bcf39d90-abfc-4239-a9e4-58942daddaca" />

I’ve also included an “about” page in which users can learn about floriography. 

<img width="754" height="691" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a264248c-c4e6-4950-b6c6-c0613278ab71" />

Seeing their personal garden grow along with them, users will surely not feel intimidated, but excited to consistently journal every single day.
